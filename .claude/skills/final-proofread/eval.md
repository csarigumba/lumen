# Final proofread eval

Answer each check with pass or fail. If any check fails, fix the post before returning it.

## Voice and content

1. Does every anecdote, detail, example, and feeling come from Sid (his answers, dictation, or an earlier post), with nothing invented?
2. Does the post keep Sid's point, without adding claims, stats, or opinions?
3. Does it reuse his own phrases where they are clear?
4. Would Sid recognize it as his own voice: plain words, short paragraphs, direct, no polish?
5. Is the amount of cutting proportional to the actual slop, leaving strong human sentences alone?
6. Is it in active voice, with human subjects where possible?

## Patterns

1. Are binary contrasts ("not X, it's Y") gone?
2. Are fake-profound kickers at the end of paragraphs and sections deleted, not rewritten into better ones?
3. Are dramatic fragments folded into real sentences?
4. Is interpretive metadiscourse ("that's the dangerous part") removed?
5. Are throat-clearing openers, faux-insight setups, and colon reveals removed?
6. Is importance puffery reduced to Sid's real strength of claim?
7. Are banned words and empty adverbs gone?
8. Are there no em dashes at all?
9. Do paragraphs vary in shape, instead of repeating setup, contrast, kicker?
10. Does the ending land on a concrete practice, action, or note, not a recap or aphorism?

## Final read

1. Read it aloud. Does any line sound like a poster or a LinkedIn post? Fix it.
2. Does `scan.py` show no em dashes or banned words, with every remaining flag reviewed?
3. Are the frontmatter, quotes, and image unchanged?
4. Does the report include a short **What changed** list and anything cut that Sid might want back?
